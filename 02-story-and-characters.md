# Story world, characters and rewards

## Premise
The worlds used to overflow with elaborate rides, strange attractions, and surprising inventions. Over many generations, people stopped making novel things. The world became **sleepy**, **predictable**, and a little **gray**. It isn't a disaster to be rescued from; it's a funny, poignant invitation to bring joy back.

An old Spark Machine has been dormant. Pip, an enthusiastic inventor, discovers that the newly arrived player can activate it. The player is a **World Maker**. The first destination is an amusement park with a coaster so slow everyone has forgotten what thrills feel like.

## First-session story arc
1. **Wonder:** glimpse wonderful worlds that are now muted.
2. **Invitation:** the player's presence activates a mysterious light.
3. **Connection:** Pip meets the player and reveals his hopeful, slightly awkward ambitions.
4. **Need:** three friends at a slow ride have different desires; one longs for excitement, one worries about it, one loves jokes.
5. **Agency:** player chooses how to transform the ride.
6. **Payoff:** anticipation, dramatic character reactions, comedy, and the park visually reigniting.
7. **Tangible reward:** a guaranteed Play Spark, perhaps a rare laughter/excitement Spark.
8. **Attachment:** equip a silly accessory; observe that same character wearing it on the next ride.
9. **Return:** another request or unexpected character interaction invites another build.

## Characters: curated initial cast
**Pip — guide and aspiring inventor.** Eager and imaginative, but his previous inventions have rarely worked. He needs the player's collaboration. His comic flaw is overenthusiasm; his emotional arc is moving from frustration to renewed confidence. He must be more than a tooltip in a costume.

**Cautious rider — provisional role, no final name.** Wants to be brave and feel excitement but fears loss of control. A readable expressive arc: apprehension → anticipation → squeal → delighted request for another ride. Avoid making fear a humiliating punchline.

**Thrill-seeker — provisional role, no final name.** Wants speed and bigger surprises; contrasts with the cautious rider. Can react intensely while also occasionally discovering an unexpected fear.

**Comedian / mischievous friend — provisional role, no final name.** Finds absurdity in everything. Loves visual gags, unconventional props and physical comedy.

The cast roles have been established, but these are **not** finalized character design sheets, dialogue recordings, or 3D assets.

## Character development principles
- Give each recurring character a clear desire, recognizable silhouette and distinctive behavior.
- Characters should change subtly as they play alongside the child; don't reset their emotion to zero every scene.
- Support expressive idle behaviors, anticipation, failure/recovery, celebration and accessory-specific gags.
- Prefer character chemistry and moments of vulnerability over constant praise.
- Show reactions through staging, pose and timing rather than relying exclusively on text.

## Sparks: collectible economy
- **Play Sparks:** regular, guaranteed after the first experienced ride of a new meaningful creation (exact duplicate-detection rules TBD).
- **Rare emotion Sparks:** e.g., Laughter Spark, Excitement Spark; intermittent unexpected rewards. Their rarity is a property of *which* Spark appears, not whether the child gets rewarded at all.
- **Unlocks (phase one):** hats, silly glasses, capes, propellers, cosmetic colorways, reaction-linked accessories.
- **Later possibilities:** new experiences, tools or worlds. Intentionally deferred.
- **No subjective creative grading.** The game should never claim the child's ride failed an AI quality test.

## Sample script, first experience
Pip: “Wait… you made that light glow? We've been waiting for a World Maker!”

Cautious rider: “Maybe the coaster could be a *little* more exciting. Just a little.”

Pip: “What if we added a hill? Or something completely ridiculous?”

[Player adds a hill. The ride starts. Cautious rider squeals; the comedian's hat flies up then lands on Pip.]

Cautious rider: “That was terrifying… CAN WE GO AGAIN?”

[Play Spark floats into a collector; the surrounding park lights turn on.]

Pip: “A Spark! Your ideas really do wake this place up!”

*All script is exploratory, not approved dialogue.*
